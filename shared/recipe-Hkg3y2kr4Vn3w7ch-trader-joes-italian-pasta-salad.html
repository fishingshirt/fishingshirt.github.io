Trader Joe’s Italian Pasta Salad

Snack ⏱ 30 🍽 6

Trader Joe’s Pasta Salad

Ingredients

  • - 1 lb box short pasta (rotini, penne, or farfalle work well)
  • - ½ small red onion, thinly sliced
  • - 8 oz tub fresh mozzarella balls (ciliegine), halved, with all their brine
  • - 1 jar pepperoncini (12–16 oz), drained, plus a splash of the brine
  • - 4–6 oz salami, sliced into thin strips
  • - 4–6 oz pepperoni, sliced into thin strips
  • - 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • - ½ cup grated Parmesan
  • - Italian seasoning, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes to taste

Instructions

  1. 1. **Cook the pasta.** Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta according to package directions until al dente. Drain and rinse under cold water to stop cooking. Let cool completely.
  2. 2. **Prep the vegetables and meats.** While the pasta cools, thinly slice the red onion, halve the cherry tomatoes, and cut the salami and pepperoni into thin strips.
  3. 3. **Halve the mozzarella.** Cut the mozzarella balls in half and reserve all the brine from the tub.
  4. 4. **Assemble the salad.** In a large bowl, combine the cooled pasta, red onion, halved mozzarella, all the reserved brine, pepperoncini plus a splash of brine, salami, pepperoni, cherry tomatoes, and grated Parmesan. *Note: using the full tub of brine will make the salad fairly wet — that’s the intended texture, but pour in gradually if you prefer it drier.*
  5. 5. **Season and toss.** Add Italian seasoning, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes to taste. Mix well until everything is evenly coated.
  6. 6. **Rest and serve.** Let sit for 15–30 minutes at room temperature, or refrigerate up to 2 hours before serving. Toss again just before serving.
  7. ### Optional add-ins
  8. - **Artichoke hearts** — quartered, drained
  9. - **Fresh basil** — torn or chopped, added just before serving
  10. - **Red wine vinegar** — 1–2 tablespoons for extra tang
